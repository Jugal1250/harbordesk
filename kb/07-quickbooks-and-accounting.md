# QuickBooks and accounting integrations

We support **QuickBooks Online** and **Xero** on the Growth and Scale plans. Connect under
**Settings → Integrations → Accounting** and authorise with your accounting login.

Once connected:
- Paid invoices sync to your accounting system nightly, or immediately if you select
  **Sync now**.
- Each customer is matched by email address. Unmatched customers appear in a review queue
  rather than creating duplicates.
- Tax codes are mapped in Integrations → Accounting → Tax mapping. Map these before the first
  sync or entries land in the default code.

Starter plans can export a QuickBooks-compatible CSV instead, from Billing → Invoices → Export.
