# Inviting teammates to your workspace

Go to **Settings → Members** and select **Invite people** in the top right. Enter one email
per line to invite several people at once, choose a role, then select **Send invites**.

Roles:
- **Admin** — full access, including billing and member management.
- **Member** — can create and edit jobs, but cannot see billing.
- **Viewer** — read-only.

Invitations expire after 7 days. To resend one, open Members, find the pending row and
select **Resend**. Only Admins can invite.

Each accepted invitation uses one seat on your plan. If you are at your seat limit, the
invite is held until a seat frees up or you add seats under Settings → Billing.
