# Cancelling or pausing your subscription

**Cancel**: Settings → Billing → **Cancel subscription**. The account stays active until the
end of the paid period, then becomes read-only for 30 days before deletion. Take a full
account export first (see Exporting your data).

**Pause**: seasonal businesses can pause for 1 to 3 months on Growth and Scale.
Settings → Billing → **Pause subscription**. Billing stops, data is kept, and the workspace is
read-only while paused. Pausing is not available on Starter.

Cancelling does not automatically refund the current period. If you were charged after
cancelling, contact support with the invoice number; charges taken after a confirmed
cancellation are refunded in full within 5 working days.
