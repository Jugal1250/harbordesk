# Updating your payment method

Go to **Settings → Billing → Payment method** and select **Update card**. The change
applies to the next charge immediately; there is no need to contact support.

If a payment has already failed, your account moves to **past due**. We retry the charge on
days 3, 5 and 7 after the failure. Updating the card triggers an immediate retry, and the
account returns to active as soon as that succeeds.

Accounts remain fully usable for 14 days while past due. After 14 days the workspace becomes
read-only until payment succeeds. No data is deleted.

Only Admins can see or change billing details.
