# Plans, seats and changing plan

| Plan | Price per seat | Seat limit | Notable features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $24/month | 10 | Core scheduling, CSV export |
| Growth | $30/month | 30 | Integrations, accounting sync, pause |
| Scale | $40/month | Unlimited | SSO/SCIM, white-label portal, priority support |

**Upgrades** take effect immediately and are prorated for the rest of the billing period.
**Downgrades** take effect at renewal so you keep what you paid for. Features above the new
plan's limit become read-only at renewal rather than being deleted.

Adding seats mid-term is prorated and keeps any annual discount already applied. Removing
seats reduces the next invoice, not the current one.
