# VAT, GST and tax on invoices

Tax is calculated from the **billing country** on your account, not the country of the card.
To correct the rate applied to your invoices, update the billing address under
**Settings → Billing → Billing details**.

- EU customers with a valid VAT number are charged at 0% under the reverse charge. Add the
  number in Billing details; it is validated against VIES within a few minutes.
- Swiss customers are charged Swiss VAT at 8.1%.
- UK customers are charged 20% unless a valid VAT number is supplied.
- Indian customers are charged 18% GST; a GSTIN can be added in Billing details.

If past invoices used the wrong rate, contact support with the invoice numbers. We issue a
credit note and a corrected invoice. Credit notes appear under Billing → Invoices within
two working days.
